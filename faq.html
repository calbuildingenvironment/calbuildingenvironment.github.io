FAQs

Common Questions

What is your inspection process?

Our process has four steps: request a quote, schedule your inspection, on-site sampling and testing, and a detailed report with accredited laboratory results. From your first call to the final report, we keep the process efficient and transparent.

How quickly can you schedule an inspection?

We offer 24-hour response on scheduling requests, and we work to accommodate real estate transaction timelines, renovation schedules, and other time-sensitive projects.

When is an asbestos inspection required?

An asbestos inspection is commonly required before renovation or demolition projects involving older buildings. We perform independent inspections, collect representative samples when appropriate, and submit them to accredited laboratories for analysis.

Do you perform mold remediation?

No. We provide independent mold inspections and environmental consulting only. Because we do not perform remediation, our findings remain objective and free from conflicts of interest.

Do you work with real estate agents and escrow transactions?

Yes. We regularly work with homeowners, buyers, sellers, and real estate professionals to provide independent inspection reports during property transactions.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Orange County, Los Angeles County, Riverside County, and San Bernardino County, California.

What types of properties do you inspect?

We inspect residential homes, apartment buildings, office buildings, retail properties, schools, healthcare facilities, industrial facilities, warehouses, government buildings, and other commercial properties throughout Southern California.

Are laboratory tests included?

When sampling is appropriate, representative samples are submitted to accredited environmental laboratories for professional analysis. Laboratory findings are included within your final report.

How long does the inspection process take?

Inspection times vary depending on the size, complexity, and scope of the project. We'll provide an estimated schedule when your inspection is booked.

What services do you provide?

Our services include asbestos inspections, mold inspections, lead-based paint inspections, indoor air quality assessments, air monitoring, air clearance testing, moisture investigations, and environmental consulting.

Still Have Questions?

If you need additional information about our environmental inspection services, contact California Building Environment today. We're happy to discuss your project and recommend the appropriate inspection services.
