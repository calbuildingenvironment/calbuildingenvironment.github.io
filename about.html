About California Building Environment

Independent Environmental Consulting Throughout Southern California

California Building Environment provides professional environmental inspections, accredited laboratory sampling, and objective consulting services for residential, commercial, industrial, educational, healthcare, and government properties.

Who We Are

Providing Objective Environmental Information You Can Trust

California Building Environment specializes in independent environmental inspections designed to help property owners, contractors, architects, engineers, facility managers, and public agencies make informed decisions before renovation, demolition, property transactions, and environmental remediation.

Because we do not perform remediation or laboratory testing, our inspections remain objective and focused solely on identifying environmental conditions and documenting findings through professional reporting and accredited laboratory analysis.

Our work includes asbestos inspections, mold inspections, lead-based paint inspections, indoor air quality assessments, air monitoring, air clearance testing, and environmental consulting throughout Southern California.
