Who We Are

A Family-Owned Firm Built on Independence and Integrity

California Building Environment is a family-owned and operated inspection firm specializing in independent environmental inspections. We help property owners, contractors, architects, engineers, facility managers, and public agencies make informed decisions before renovation, demolition, property transactions, and environmental remediation.

Because we are family-owned and never perform remediation or laboratory testing, our inspections remain objective and focused solely on identifying environmental conditions and documenting findings through professional reporting and accredited laboratory analysis.

Our work includes asbestos inspections, mold inspections, lead-based paint inspections, indoor air quality assessments, air monitoring, air clearance testing, and environmental consulting throughout Southern California.
