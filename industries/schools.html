Schools & Districts

Testing & Inspection for Schools & Districts

School facilities carry specific regulatory obligations under AHERA, and any abatement work performed on campus requires TEM clearance before students and staff can return to the space.

Request a Quote

What to Know

  • AHERA requires periodic reinspection of known and assumed asbestos-containing materials in school buildings.
  • Abatement projects on school campuses require TEM clearance testing, not PCM, before reoccupancy.
  • We can work around the academic calendar to minimize disruption to instructional time.
