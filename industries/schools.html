Schools & Districts

Testing & Inspection for Schools & Districts

School facilities carry specific regulatory obligations under AHERA, and any abatement work performed on campus requires TEM clearance before students and staff can return to the space.

What to Know

  • AHERA requires periodic reinspection of known and assumed asbestos-containing materials in school buildings.
  • Abatement projects on school campuses require TEM clearance testing, not PCM, before reoccupancy.
  • We can work around the academic calendar to minimize disruption to instructional time.

AHERA Compliance Documentation You Can Put in the File

Public and private schools carry obligations under AHERA that most other buildings do not. Districts must maintain an asbestos management plan, perform triennial re-inspections, and document response actions — and the paperwork has to survive review by state and federal authorities.

We work with school districts and facility staff to complete the pieces that belong in the compliance file: original inspections of suspect materials, periodic re-inspections on the required schedule, and the sampling that supports informed response decisions. When abatement work occurs, TEM air clearance testing verifies the area is safe for students and staff to reoccupy.

Because schools cannot pause instruction, we schedule sampling and clearance around breaks, evenings, and weekends whenever possible. Reports are formatted for district compliance files and are ready to share with consultants, contractors, and oversight agencies.
