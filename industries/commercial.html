Commercial Testing That Fits Around Your Business

Tenant improvements, leasehold remodels, and building repositioning all share a common question: if the building predates the mid-1980s, does it contain asbestos or lead that needs to be identified before permits can move? What sets commercial testing apart is the environment — occupied spaces, operating tenants, security schedules, and lease deadlines that do not pause for sampling.

We plan inspections around your building's rhythm. Representative samples are collected in focused sessions coordinated with your property manager or facilities team so business disruption stays minimal. When an area is already vacant — an empty unit, a shell space, a floor between tenants — we can schedule larger surveys efficiently.

Commercial owners and investors also rely on inspection documentation for insurance, lender, and due-diligence requirements. Our reports compile findings and laboratory results into a single document you can share with lenders, insurers, architects, and contractors.
