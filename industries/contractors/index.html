Survey Results on Your Schedule, Not the Other Way Around

General contractors and specialty trades carry the risk of what is inside the buildings they work in. An undiscovered asbestos floor tile or pipe insulation found mid-demolition can mean a stop-work order, a change order, or a dispute with the owner about who pays. The fix is knowing before you mobilize.

We deliver survey results when contractors need them: before you finalize a bid, before demolition, or before your crew arrives on site. Pre-bid surveys give you the confidence to price a job accurately; pre-demolition surveys satisfy the permitting requirements many California cities and air districts enforce before work begins.

Our documentation is built for the field. The report identifies suspect materials by location, notes the samples collected, and includes laboratory results you can attach to your submittals and keep on file. For projects under SCAQMD, Cal/OSHA, or other regulatory oversight, the inspection record supports your compliance paperwork.
