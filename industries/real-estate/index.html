Due Diligence Without Stalling Escrow

For buyers, sellers, and their agents, environmental testing is a time-sensitive step in a transaction that already has enough deadlines. An asbestos or lead inspection requested early can be completed and reported within days, giving everyone the information they need before contingencies expire.

Sellers often test proactively — a pre-1978 home with a clean lead report and an asbestos survey on file removes a common negotiation point before offers come in. Buyers order inspections to understand the true condition of what they are purchasing, especially when a renovation is planned. In both cases, an independent third-party report keeps the transaction moving with facts instead of speculation.

We coordinate directly with the parties who need results: the buyer's agent, the escrow officer, the title company, or the lender. Reports are delivered in a straightforward format agents can pass along and lenders can file, with results stated clearly and no remediation bias — we do not perform the work, so there is no conflict in our findings.
