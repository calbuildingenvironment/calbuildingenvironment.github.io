What to Know

  • Popcorn ceilings, vinyl floor tile, and stucco are common suspect materials in homes built before the 1990s.
  • Many California cities require an asbestos survey before issuing a demolition or major renovation permit.
  • Pre-1978 homes are subject to federal lead paint disclosure requirements at time of sale.
