Portfolio-Wide Testing on a Schedule That Makes Sense

Property management companies juggle many buildings, many owners, and many maintenance calendars — environmental testing works best when it is consolidated and predictable. We help managers plan asbestos surveys before capital improvement projects, lead testing during unit turnovers, and clearance sampling after work wraps up.

For portfolios, we can coordinate multiple units or buildings into an efficient series of site visits, so your staff spends minutes coordinating rather than hours. Unit-turnover testing — especially in pre-1978 buildings with tile or popcorn ceilings — protects the owner, the next tenant, and your company from surprise liabilities.

Each report is owner-ready: findings, sample locations, laboratory results, and plain-language recommendations. Property managers can forward them directly to property owners, insurance carriers, or contractors. When tenant concerns about mold or indoor air quality arise, an independent inspection gives you a defensible record of what was checked and what was found.
