California Building Environment provides independent environmental inspections, professional sample collection, and environmental consulting for residential, commercial, industrial, healthcare, educational, and government clients throughout Los Angeles County.

Professional Environmental Inspection Services

Whether your project involves renovation, demolition, commercial property management, environmental due diligence, or indoor environmental concerns, California Building Environment delivers independent inspections backed by accredited laboratory analysis.

We perform detailed inspections and collect representative samples when appropriate. Samples are submitted to accredited laboratories for analysis, and laboratory findings are incorporated into comprehensive inspection reports.

  • Asbestos Inspections
  • Mold Inspections
  • Lead-Based Paint Inspections
  • Indoor Air Quality Assessments
  • Air Monitoring
  • Air Clearance Testing
