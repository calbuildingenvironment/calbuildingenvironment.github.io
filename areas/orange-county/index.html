Serving Orange County

Independent Environmental Consulting for Orange County Projects

From coastal communities where marine-layer humidity drives moisture and mold concerns to inland cities with pre-1978 housing stock requiring lead and asbestos evaluations, Orange County presents diverse environmental inspection needs. We provide independent inspections tailored to your project — whether it's a high-rise renovation in Irvine Spectrum, a school modernization in Santa Ana, a warehouse conversion in Anaheim, or a residential remodel in Laguna Beach.

Inspections follow AHERA, NESHAP, Cal/OSHA, EPA RRP, and HUD protocols as applicable. Samples are submitted to NVLAP-accredited (asbestos), AIHA-LAP, LLC accredited (mold, lead, air), and ELAP-certified laboratories. Findings are documented in detailed reports with material locations, condition assessments, analytical results, and regulatory classifications.

  • Asbestos Inspections — NESHAP, AHERA, Cal/OSHA compliance
  • Mold Inspections — IICRC S520, moisture investigations
  • Lead-Based Paint Inspections — EPA RRP, HUD Guidelines
  • Indoor Air Quality Assessments — ASHRAE 62.1, OSHA/Cal/OSHA
  • Air Monitoring — PCM (NIOSH 7400), TEM (AHERA/ISO 10312)
  • Air Clearance Testing — AHERA TEM, PCM, project-specific

Asbestos Inspections

Pre-renovation/demolition surveys for OC's commercial high-rises, schools, hospitals, and pre-1980 housing. NVLAP-accredited PLM/TEM lab analysis per EPA 600/R-93/116. NESHAP, AHERA, Cal/OSHA compliance.

Mold Inspections

Coastal humidity, slab leaks, roof failures, and HVAC condensation drive mold in OC properties. Visual + moisture mapping, IR thermography, spore trap & surface sampling. AIHA-LAP lab analysis per IICRC S520.

Lead-Based Paint Inspections

Pre-1978 housing in Anaheim, Santa Ana, Orange, Fullerton, and coastal communities. XRF screening + confirmatory paint chip sampling per HUD Ch.7 / EPA 40 CFR 745. AIHA-LAP lab analysis.

Indoor Air Quality

IAQ assessments for OC offices, schools, healthcare, and multifamily: ventilation (CO₂, ASHRAE 62.1), VOCs (TO-15), particulate (PM2.5), mold spores, formaldehyde. AIHA-LAP/ELAP labs.

Air Monitoring

Daily PCM/TEM monitoring during asbestos abatement at OC schools, hospitals, high-rises, and infrastructure projects. Perimeter, containment, and clearance sampling per AHERA, Cal/OSHA, NESHAP, SCAQMD Rule 1403.

Air Clearance Testing

AHERA TEM clearance (≤70 s/mm²) for K-12 schools; PCM/TEM for commercial, industrial, and residential abatement. Post-remediation mold (IICRC S520) and lead (HUD Ch.15) clearance available.
