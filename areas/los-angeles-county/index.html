Serving Los Angeles County

Professional Environmental Inspection Services for LA County

Los Angeles County's scale and diversity — from downtown high-rises and port industrial zones to San Fernando Valley residential tracts and Antelope Valley commercial corridors — demands inspections that account for local building stock, municipal amendments (LADBS, LACFD), and regional environmental conditions. We provide independent inspections for renovation, demolition, property transactions, and compliance projects across LA County.

Inspections follow AHERA, NESHAP, Cal/OSHA, EPA RRP, HUD, SCAQMD Rule 1403, and LADBS requirements. Samples submitted to NVLAP (asbestos), AIHA-LAP, LLC (mold, lead, air), and ELAP labs. Reports include material locations, condition assessments, analytical results, regulatory classifications, and permit-ready documentation.

  • Asbestos Inspections — NESHAP, AHERA, Cal/OSHA, LADBS compliance
  • Mold Inspections — IICRC S520, moisture investigations
  • Lead-Based Paint Inspections — EPA RRP, HUD, LADBS
  • Indoor Air Quality Assessments — ASHRAE 62.1, OSHA/Cal/OSHA
  • Air Monitoring — PCM (NIOSH 7400), TEM (AHERA/ISO 10312)
  • Air Clearance Testing — AHERA TEM, PCM, project-specific

Asbestos Inspections

Pre-renovation/demolition surveys for LA's diverse building stock: downtown high-rises, pre-1978 apartments, industrial corridors (Vernon, Commerce), port facilities, schools (LAUSD), hospitals. NVLAP PLM/TEM per EPA 600/R-93/116. LADBS, NESHAP, AHERA, Cal/OSHA compliance.

Mold Inspections

Coastal marine layer, aging plumbing, slab leaks, and high-rise HVAC drive mold in LA County. Visual + moisture mapping, IR thermography, spore trap & surface sampling. AIHA-LAP lab analysis per IICRC S520.

Lead-Based Paint Inspections

Vast pre-1978 housing stock across LA County: East LA, South LA, San Fernando Valley, San Gabriel Valley, coastal cities. XRF + paint chip sampling per HUD Ch.7 / EPA 40 CFR 745. AIHA-LAP lab. LADBS, EPA RRP, HUD compliance.

Los Angeles County lead paint inspections →

Indoor Air Quality

IAQ for LA offices, schools (LAUSD), healthcare, studios, warehouses, ports: ventilation (CO₂, ASHRAE 62.1), VOCs (TO-15), PM2.5, mold spores, formaldehyde. AIHA-LAP/ELAP labs. Title 24, Cal/OSHA compliance.

Los Angeles County indoor air quality assessments →

Air Monitoring

Daily PCM/TEM monitoring during asbestos abatement at LAUSD schools, Metro projects, hospitals, high-rises, port terminals, and industrial sites. Perimeter, containment, clearance per AHERA, Cal/OSHA 1529, NESHAP, SCAQMD Rule 1403.

Air Clearance Testing

AHERA TEM clearance (≤70 s/mm²) for LAUSD K-12 schools; PCM/TEM for commercial, industrial, residential, and Metro projects. Post-remediation mold (IICRC S520) and lead (HUD Ch.15) clearance available.
