Serving San Bernardino County

Professional Environmental Inspection Services for San Bernardino County

California's largest county by area — from Ontario/Rancho Cucamonga logistics hubs and High Desert solar/wind corridors to Big Bear mountain resorts, Victor Valley residential, and San Bernardino/Redlands institutional campuses — requires inspections that span vast distances and diverse building types. We provide independent inspections for public works, logistics, healthcare, education, and residential projects across San Bernardino County.

Inspections follow AHERA, NESHAP, Cal/OSHA, EPA RRP, HUD, MDAQMD, and SCAQMD requirements. Samples to NVLAP (asbestos), AIHA-LAP, LLC (mold, lead, air), ELAP labs. Reports with material locations, conditions, analytical results, regulatory classifications.

  • Asbestos Inspections — NESHAP, AHERA, Cal/OSHA compliance
  • Mold Inspections — IICRC S520, moisture investigations
  • Lead-Based Paint Inspections — EPA RRP, HUD Guidelines
  • Indoor Air Quality Assessments — ASHRAE 62.1, OSHA/Cal/OSHA
  • Air Monitoring — PCM (NIOSH 7400), TEM (AHERA/ISO 10312)
  • Air Clearance Testing — AHERA TEM, PCM, project-specific

Asbestos Inspections

Pre-renovation/demolition surveys for SB County logistics warehouses (Ontario, Rancho, Fontana), High Desert solar/wind, Big Bear resorts, Victor Valley housing, SB/Redlands schools and hospitals. NVLAP PLM/TEM per EPA 600/R-93/116. NESHAP, AHERA, Cal/OSHA, MDAQMD/SCAQMD compliance.

Mold Inspections

Mountain snowmelt, High Desert flash floods, slab leaks, and warehouse roof failures drive mold. Visual + moisture mapping, IR thermography, spore trap & surface sampling. AIHA-LAP lab per IICRC S520.

Lead-Based Paint Inspections

Pre-1978 housing in San Bernardino, Redlands, Ontario, Fontana, Victor Valley, Big Bear. XRF + paint chip sampling per HUD Ch.7 / EPA 40 CFR 745. AIHA-LAP lab. EPA RRP, HUD compliance.

Indoor Air Quality

IAQ for SB County logistics warehouses, hospitals, schools, mountain resorts, High Desert solar: ventilation (CO₂, ASHRAE 62.1), VOCs (TO-15), PM2.5, mold spores, formaldehyde. AIHA-LAP/ELAP labs.

Air Monitoring

Daily PCM/TEM monitoring during asbestos abatement at SB County schools, hospitals, logistics warehouses, High Desert energy projects, and mountain resorts. Perimeter, containment, clearance per AHERA, Cal/OSHA, NESHAP, MDAQMD/SCAQMD.
