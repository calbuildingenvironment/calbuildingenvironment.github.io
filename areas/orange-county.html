California Building Environment provides independent environmental inspections, professional sample collection, and environmental consulting for residential, commercial, industrial, healthcare, educational, and government clients throughout Orange County.

Independent Environmental Consulting You Can Trust

Whether your project involves renovation, demolition, property acquisition, environmental due diligence, or indoor environmental concerns, California Building Environment provides professional inspections supported by accredited laboratory analysis.

We perform inspections and collect representative samples when appropriate. Samples are submitted to accredited laboratories for analysis, and laboratory findings are incorporated into our detailed inspection reports.

  • Mold Inspections
