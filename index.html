Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Answers to the questions we receive most often about environmental inspections and testing.

Do I need a mold inspection?

A mold inspection is recommended when there are signs of moisture, visible mold growth, musty odors, or concerns about indoor air quality.

When is an asbestos inspection required?

Asbestos inspections are commonly performed before renovation, demolition, property transactions, or when asbestos-containing materials are suspected.

How long does an inspection take?

Most residential inspections are completed within a few hours, while larger commercial projects vary depending on the size and scope.

When will I receive my report?

Reports are typically delivered after laboratory analysis is complete and reviewed by our inspection team.
