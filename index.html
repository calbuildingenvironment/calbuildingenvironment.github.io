Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Answers to the questions we receive most often about environmental inspections and testing.

When is an asbestos inspection required?

Asbestos inspections are commonly required before renovation or demolition of older buildings, during property transactions, or when asbestos-containing materials are suspected.

Do I need a mold inspection?

A mold inspection is recommended when there are signs of moisture, visible mold growth, musty odors, or concerns about indoor air quality.

How long does an inspection take?

Most single-family inspections take 2–4 hours on site; larger commercial projects are scoped to the property.

When will I receive my report?

Reports are typically delivered 5–7 business days after the inspection, once laboratory analysis is complete and reviewed by our inspection team.
